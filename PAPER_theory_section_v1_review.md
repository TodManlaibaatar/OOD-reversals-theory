## Predictions and falsifiers for existing logs

1. **Output channel at crossover — theorem-regime coefficient, prediction outside the theorem regime in E2.** Compare \(O_{2,K}^{\rm phys}/(\mu_1^2q^2T_{\rm out})\) with \(1-n\). A share of total cluster-2 harm has an additional factor \(T_{\rm out}/d_{2,*}\). The denominator for a share cannot be the total source-summed rate at its zero. Separate output, radial and noncore terms before checking. Wrong sign or a systematic normalized discrepancy rejects the canonical extrapolation. BC twoway; CPD table.
2. **Help factorization — theorem-regime control, prediction outside the theorem regime in the pilot.** The positive full \(X_1\)-weighted lag times the same core exposure predicts \(-D_1^\tau/q^2\) with normalized ratio \(1+O(q^{1/20})\). Retaining all remainder channels tests an exact identity. A reconstruction discrepancy beyond precision falsifies the bookkeeping match. A wrong-sign lag or residual comparable with help falsifies the small-remainder extrapolation. CPD help.
3. **Linear monotonicity — theorem-regime, exact.** Only after checking equal aligned weights, the Gram eigenvalues and joint data eigenbasis, inspect every nonzero probe and its antipode. A resolved increase or antipodal mismatch contradicts the exact control; positive cluster rate does so if that cluster is also diagonal. Approximate commutation, random width and nondiagonal sample moments leave this exact test. LP linear-theorem, linear-SIM.
4. **Entry inactive fraction — comparison theorem, prediction outside the trained theorem regime.** Read the same initial right-half ancestry at \(T_A=\log(A/S_0)\), weight by radial mass and use the current gate. At fixed \(A,q\), the formula with \(b=(S_0/A)^\omega/q\) increases with \(S_0\). A reversed trend or failure to approach the predicted curve at matched entry rejects the trained-entry proxy. Crossing-time bridge share cannot alone test the entry formula. RMS join.
5. **Sweep placement — exact exponent arithmetic, prediction outside the theorem regime for its trained interpretation.** At \(\rho=2/3\), \(\kappa_c=18/5\) lies inside the reported range about 2.1–5.9 [eval]. If actual logged initial norms put all sweep points on the same side, the placement is wrong. An opposite entry trend after matching \(A,q,\zeta\) rejects the split extrapolation. A discontinuity of reversal occurrence at that exponent is not predicted. RMS map, join.
6. **Early weak input sign — unreconciled, prediction outside the theorem regime.** Verify the clock, learned window, strict gate and producer/carrier split first. A negative like-for-like canonical producer term rejects the sign extrapolation. A changed mask or event time leaves the comparison unresolved. The proven positive interval is \(J\cap(-\infty,1/1000]\) in the deep family, not every early training time. CPD table; PHS transfer.

## Scope issues in condensing the sources

The mathematical statements cited in Theorems 1–3 and Proposition 4 retain their source hypotheses. Three stronger readings of the experiment-interface wording are excluded:

- LP proves monotonicity inside the commuting aligned class. Its positive-quadrant remark shows that this initialization generally leaves that class. Neither the remark nor the theorem proves that a transient occurs outside it.
- The proved factor \(1-n\) multiplies the output-channel coefficient. A universal *fraction of total harm* proportional to \(1-n\) with constant coefficient would also require control of \(T_{\rm out}/d_{2,*}\) as a constant. No such assertion is imported. The output remainder \(r_2\) includes other terms at finite noise.
- The exact help decomposition retains \(-\epsilon_B+r_1\). A product-only empirical ratio or a comparison between an entry inactive fraction and a later bridge share is not an exact transfer identity. The roadmap calls the sweep link partial; this draft labels its trained-entry use a prediction and the time/weight mismatch explicit.

These checks stop the stronger interpretations without changing a source statement. No experimental dataset or run was analyzed.

## Limitations paragraph

The deep theorem has an unevaluated \(q_0\), an \(O(q)\)-wide sector and an unspecified persistence horizon. It does not cover the reported \(176^\circ\) reversal or other persistent directions outside its sector. OP-W is the remaining weak-produced input sign. For the critical family, OP-C1 asks whether a fixed positive fraction stays inactive indefinitely; the proved survival guarantee is \(C_*/(1+T)\) on each finite zero-weak-mass resident interval. OP-C2 asks for the coupled weak passage, seed, clock, exposure and exact lag. OP-C3 asks for critical trained entry, including noise-scale angular or weighted-profile precision and tail matching. At \(q=1/20,\ S_0=1/5000\), \(20^2<5000<20^3\) gives exponent placement only. No canonical reversal time, magnitude, attribution or persistent direction is certified here. Any conditional computer-assisted local evidence has separate hypotheses and is not a premise.
