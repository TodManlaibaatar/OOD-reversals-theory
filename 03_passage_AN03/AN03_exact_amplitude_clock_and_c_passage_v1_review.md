It also gives two concrete bridges needed by that substitution: an exact amplitude-versus-response ledger and an original-state cross-output bound in terms of amplitude and transverse energy.

The growing amplitude is h=((U₂+W₂)/2)². It is not U₂² alone and is not radial chart mass. Let H_C be its integral on fixed initial labels. With Q=λ+q² and exact response c=E_P2[f₂X₂]/Q,

H_C′ = Q(1−c)H_C + D_C.

The defect D_C is displayed as an exact integral. It contains no B₁₁ term; it still contains the evolving first coordinates through off-diagonal terms. There is no division by an individual label amplitude, so a label with z=0 causes no singularity in the aggregate identity.

## The precise c-form authorization

A new proposition states the actual required field representation, rather than simply renaming N as c. It permits instantaneous strong mass M∈(0,1] and does not require M or the radial weights to obey a logistic law. The fixed-core radius argument is labelwise, and the mean reference is a characteristic in the same field.

The remainder must be controlled in value and receiver derivative. Its value enters mean forcing; its derivative enters radius growth multiplicatively. The proposition is applicable to the original flow only when that field representation and its quantitative remainder have been proved there.

The aggregate amplitude identity alone does not prove the field representation. On P2, writing f₂=cX₂+g₂ makes g₂ orthogonal to X₂ globally. It does not make g₂ orthogonal to X₂ after multiplying by a receiver’s ReLU selector. The file gives an explicit orthogonal-residual counterexample to that implication. That example is not claimed to be a reached network state.

## Endpoint and forcing qualifications

H_C is not automatically bounded by c. Their difference includes antisymmetric energy, the response of complementary labels, and an exact negative-gate term. Any valid cap H_C(t_b)≤H_b works in the passage theorem; H_b=c_b needs the ledger to justify it.

The same ledger gives c≤H_C+(c_O)_++|τ_C|, useful for integrating the c part of mean forcing once the complementary terms are controlled.

The exact cross-output bound uses E_C=H_C+A_C and transverse energy J_C. Under J_C≤q²H_* it yields an H+sqrt(HH_*) structure, but also the explicit q²H_* term. The latter can be charged separately over the sojourn. Neither J_C≤q²H_* nor A_C≲H_C is established from initialization here.

## Audit points

1. **Time normalization.** Q(1−c)/2 is subtracted from the mixture residual matrix. Differentiating z² multiplies by two and produces Q(1−c), with no extra mixture factor.
2. **Fixed labels.** No chart membership derivative is taken. Moving masks would require flux.
3. **Zeros.** The polynomial identity is valid at individual z=0. The aggregate logarithm is used only where H_C>0; preventing an aggregate zero requires its own finite lower clock-defect bound.
4. **Sign.** The integrated signed defect appears with a minus sign in the clock multiplier. Positive defect helps; its observed sign is not a proof premise.
5. **Endpoint versus subinterval hypotheses.** The passage theorem uses only the endpoint lower bound on the cumulative defect, because its comparison multiplier is monotone. The forcing-integral proposition requires a lower bound on every subinterval. These assumptions are not conflated.
6. **Response ledger.** The Gaussian negative-part term is signed and nonzero; the theorem only uses its absolute bound where stated. Antisymmetric energy enters H−U₂W₂ positively.
7. **No unnecessary logarithm.** The comparison multiplier, not the actual radius, has a positive lower logarithmic growth rate. Its Riccati integral is bounded by terminal multiplier divided by α(1−c_b).
8. **No phase substitution.** The amplitude clock does not assert that exact c obeys logistic dynamics or that c is the same variable as a leading family mass.
9. **Uniformity.** Constants and defect budgets must be uniform in the proposed q,S₀ regime to obtain a uniform theorem. A q-dependent hidden exponential in a defect would change the exponent test.
