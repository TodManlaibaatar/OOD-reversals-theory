# E5 Step 4 review · core output descent and the pre-weak minimum

**Decision.** A, B and C close for every fixed critical constant $c>0$. On every BRW-extracted logistic resident law, the core output and the full leading probe error are at most $C-k e^{Ls}/(1+s)$, with separate positive constants. For every prescribed depth there is a fixed delay after strong half mass at which the actual trained scaled error beats that depth before weak learning, for all sufficiently small noise. The proof uses the energy baseline $1/2$ and the normalized clock.

## Estimates and face checks

Below $D=e^{Ls}/(1+s)$, $L=\lambda/2$, and the measures are actual radial mass $d\mu=M\,d\pi$ and fixed normalized rank law $d\pi$. Constants in these checks are uniform over the extractions for the fixed parameters.

## Fixed-time transfer checks

- At each fixed $s$, the Cartesian bounds give $|U|,|W|\le C_s$ and $|U_2|,|W_2|\le C_sq$. Hence $|U^T\xi|\le C_s q$. A discarded initial strip of length $\delta$ contributes $O_s(\delta)$ to **both** $f_1(\xi)/q$ and $f_2(\xi)/q^2$. Compact right sectors pass by continuity of positive part, including gate equality.
- Compact Q2 is checked from a fixed small nominal cap. CRA's target law and P's angle and log-weight comparison put both trained second components above $k_Iq$ with $m=O_I(q^2)$. On the finite subsequent time interval, the full field has off-diagonals $O_s(q)$ and $R_{22}\ge-C_sq^2$. While both components are nonnegative, both derivatives are at least $-C_{s,I}q^2$. Neither component can reach $k_Iq/2$ from its entry in that time, including joint contact. The input is probe-inactive at observation.
- On a compact Q3 sector the target-only angular drift is exponentially small while both Gaussian projected means are negative with a fixed margin. Its integral up to the small cap is smaller than the distance to either enlarged endpoint, and the target log radial increment is also negligible. Actual $m=O_{s,I}(S_0)=o(q^2)$ follows by radial comparison and finite elapsed growth. The left half's coarser $o(q)$ mass estimate alone would not pay this probe error.
- For each extracted subsequence the exact scaled squared error converges to that law's $\mathcal E_\zeta(s)$. A common upper bound gives the full limsup without angular-law uniqueness. Given $K_0>0$ and fixed $\eta>0$, choose finite $s_{K_0}$ so $C-k_ED(s_{K_0})<-K_0-1$, then take $q$ small enough for the fixed-time upper bound and BRW delay. The witness belongs to $[t_h,t_\eta)$ even if the hit is infinite; no attainment of the infimum on a half-open interval is assumed. See `an04codv1:trained`.

**Dependence and interpretation.** All conclusions hold for each fixed positive $c$, fixed allowed $\rho$, and fixed $\zeta>a_\rho$. The incoming coefficient $c^\omega\tan\alpha$, protected first-moment constant $c_3$, budgets, onset times, and admissible noise cutoff can depend on these choices. No constant is claimed at $s=t_\eta-t_h$. In MPB's terminology the positive mean output of the strong population drives the core output downward; the active core exposes that change at the probe. Here the result is a trained **pre-weak** depth statement. The swing-by minimum after weak learning remains open.
