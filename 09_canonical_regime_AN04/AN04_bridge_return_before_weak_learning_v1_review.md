# Trained bridge through weak learning · partial result and refuted continuation

**Decision.** Enhancement at the trained learned minimum remains open. A fixed band that is already inactive at strong half mass cannot be carried to weak learning as a continuously inactive cohort. The trained HBD band becomes active again. This rules out one proposed way of paying the Step 1 cost. It does not show that the depth inequality fails.

**Proved in the trained model.** For each fixed critical constant, ratio and separated probe, there is a finite delay after strong half mass at which every label in the HBD band is strictly active, with a common scaled margin. This remains true up to any fixed later resident time as noise tends to zero. The weak response stays uniformly below any fixed positive learning threshold for at least a constant times the square root of log inverse noise. Continuous survivors from the right half, weighted by their original half-mass-time radial weights, have vanishing mass through the first such weak-response hit. Existence of the hit, its upper time, and the instantaneous inactive mass at that hit are open. See `an04brwv1:return`, `an04brwv1:delay`, and `an04brwv1:survivors`.

**How the return is proved.** HBD's extraction extends to every fixed finite resident horizon. A fixed band has growth ceiling strictly below the rate of sufficiently distant donors. Their first moment forces a uniformly negative doubled input velocity everywhere the fixed band is still above a lower positive gate. Both the tail barrier and its rank ceiling are proved with logistic strong mass and the trained limiting entrance, rather than importing the saturated aligned-entry theorem. A separate transverse-energy bound in the full Gaussian dynamics delays weak learning. See `an04brwv1:budget` and `an04brwv1:drift`.

**Exact failure versus unpaid test.** The false inequality is the assumption that all labels of the HBD band satisfy the inactive gate throughout the passage. At the proved return time their horizontal probe output divided by noise is bounded away from zero. For actual depth, `an04brwv1:causal` is the unpaid signed inequality for two independently evolved systems: the trained run and the half-mass run with only the bridge concentrated at the core. It includes direct and propagated changes in the full training kernel, the actual removal cost, changing residual weights, and the gap from the concentrated run to the selected coherent minimum. Its sign is undetermined. The sign for an arbitrary coherent benchmark cannot be read off from a single early velocity integral.

**Independent checks and handoff.** Equation `an04brwv1:payment` gives the equivalent all-receiver rate accounting on the factual trajectory; `an04brwv1:endpoint` gives a current-gate Gaussian projection test at a learned time. The latter separates any still-inactive part of the original band, other inactive donors, regression, and active geometry. Next one needs a reached weak-response window and either a signed bound on the propagated kernel difference or a terminal comparison of those projection terms. All results here are analytic; the finite canonical run is outside their coverage.
