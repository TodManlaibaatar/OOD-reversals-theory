## Thresholds and placement

Put \(u=169/400,\ v=49/100,\ j(t)=2/t+4/(1-t)\), \(L_-=2+169/231,\ L_+=250/97+49/51\). In order, the rational enclosures for the six comparison thresholds are

- \(1/\omega\in[800/231,200/51]\), interior Q1 scale;
- \(2/\lambda\in[200/49,800/169]\), interior Q2 axis crossing;
- \(2/\omega\in[1600/231,400/51]\), last Q1 chart arrival;
- \(2/[\lambda(1-\lambda)]\in[2/(v(1-v)),2/(u(1-u))]\), compact Q2 reaching a fixed Q1 angle;
- \(4/\lambda\in[400/49,1600/169]\), last Q2 crossing;
- \(j(\lambda)\in[j(u),j(v)]\), bulk Q2 strong-chart arrival.

The two candidate passage tests have thresholds \(2s/(1-P)\in(2L_-,2L_+)\) and \(8s/[3(1-P)]\in(8L_-/3,8L_+/3)\). The band \(2<\kappa<3\) is below all eight. Ten is above all except \(j(\lambda)\). At the critical scale \(\kappa_q=1/\omega-\log c/\ell\), so \((S_0/A)^\omega/q=b=(c/A)^\omega\). The candidate expressions give \(\epsilon(T_A)=(c/A)^d\) and \(E_{\rm loc}(1/\omega)=-P\). Their algebra is exact, their dynamical use conditional. The initialized result at ten uses the proved deep chain. [CRA thresholds, ledger; CFS scope, original; RTA theoremA]

## Composition audit

1. **Join and normalize** (an04rmsv1:join). The Q1 and Q4 sectors have the same comparison model, initial label law and \(T_A\). Each contributes \(A/8\). After dividing by \(A/4\) the Gaussian right-half active fraction is \([1+F_b(H(\zeta))]/2\), whereas the point-data fraction at \(\zeta>a\) is \([1+F_b(\zeta)]/2\). No shared endpoint has mass. Q2/Q3 are excluded. Multiplying the normalized measure by \(M_0\) gives the precise leading data required by GOE or IMP. It supplies no trained entry clock. [CFS laws, active, profiles; RPS q4, fraction; GOE integration]
2. **Match horizons and ranks** (an04rmsv1:horizons). At the prescribed \(M_0=1,N=0\) state, GOE and IMP use the same finite first moments, ordered continuous upper tail and positive initial mean. GOE's obstruction fixes the segment as the time horizon grows. IMP's survivor construction changes the selected entry radius with the horizon. Thus both statements apply together, even at \(T=2\ell/\lambda\) in the continued \(N=0\) model. [GOE ceiling, escape, gap; IMP scope, budget, mass]

**Composition gaps.** Absolute early errors at fixed cap do not transfer the comparison profile at \(o(q)\) precision. Initial normalization does not evolve the trained mass to \(M=1\). Clamping preserves an entry gate fraction and has costed entry moments, but a clamped trajectory is a separate field. Initial gate signs and short-time escape do not classify perpetual survival. Equating elapsed resident time to a logistic seed clock does not run a weak passage. Instantaneous forced rates do not give passage budgets, and leading-system observables do not provide original signed lag or probe-rate precision. [CFS original, sat; RPS rank, cone, face; GOE clock, failure; IMP mass, weak, ceiling, handoff]

## Named open problems

**OP-W.** Show \(X>Y\) from \(1/1000\) through \(\sup J\) on the selected branches if this interval is nonempty. A first zero at \(X=Y\) needs \(\lambda n(V-Y)\Phi(\rho Y)+Y+nK(U)>(1-n)\rho K(\rho Y)\) for a strict inward test. The positive-\(T\) estimate used a pre-half-mass region. The attempted nonnegative quadrant lacked lower entry and inward face estimates for \(Y\). The rational local extension stops at \(1/1000\). A solution fills the remaining interpretive producer sign in deep B-core. The four-part identity and Theorem A already hold. [CPD missing; PHS missing, theorem, transfer]

**OP-C1.** Determine whether the infimum over \(T\) of mass initially inactive and still inactive at every time through \(T\) is positive, by entry parameters. The moving barrier yields only a vanishing lower bound. The wedge requires \(\rho S(x)-Y\ge0\) at the initially aligned face, where the actual sign is negative. At \(G=\rho K(\rho x)-Y=0\) a second test needs \(2\lambda\Phi(\rho x)U+Y\ge\rho K_s\) and a separate input gate condition. A solution classifies perpetual positive-mass survival. [IMP mass, resident; GOE failure]

**OP-C2.** Match a weak seed, clock and strong/weak state and continue their coupled donor fields through weak half mass. Required observables include exposure, compensation, signed lag and rate-weighted producer terms. Candidate seed scaling and the passive clock do not give this passage. The forced ceiling requires an actual integral bound, and negative-input contraction lacks the required boundary entry and matching data. A solution supplies the leading weak-learning evolution of the split state. [CRA contraction, handoff; GOE clock; IMP weak, matrix, handoff]

**OP-C3.** Establish the initialized critical strong-entry clock, \(o(q)\) angular or sufficient weighted-profile precision, radial and remaining-sector budgets, saturation transfer and outer matching. The early cap's \(O(\delta)\) allowance exceeds the angular gap scale. ## Scope exceptions found

- Roadmap §5.1 interprets negative \(E_{\rm loc}\) as driving the shape to order one. The cited conditional small-shape calculation bounds an upper envelope. Its failure cannot prove the lower mass or spread bound for an initialized bridge. The synthesis stops that inference. [CRA map, thresholds; CFS spread, original]
- A broad reading of the roadmap's assessment that escape cannot build the split exceeds GOE gap. Its obstruction requires sustained exponential growth of a fixed positive upper segment with distinct continuous ranks. Finite-horizon outward crossing and slower motion remain compatible. The synthesis stops the unrestricted inference. [GOE gap, failure; RPS cone, moved]
- The critical profile's zero-noise scale factor is not the positive-noise scaled angle. The roadmap's §2.3 caveat and CFS identify the difference explicitly. [CFS laws, positive]
