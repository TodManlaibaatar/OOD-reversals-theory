\paragraph{Trained flow and comparison.}
Use P data, representation, init and residualmatrix. Time is normalized;
$13/20\le\rho\le7/10$, $\lambda=\rho^2$, $\omega=(1-\lambda)/2$,
$S_0=cq^{1/\omega}$, $c$ in a fixed positive compact. Write
$a_*=1/2+q^2$, $b_*=\lambda/2+q^2$,
$H=\sqrt{S_0}e^{a_*t}$, $E=e^{-\omega t}+q$.
Use $H^2\le\delta_0$ for a fixed sufficiently small numerical cap,
$q$ sufficiently small, and only times from initialization. The symbol
$H_b$ below denotes the CFS map called $H$ in that paper.
Hats refer to the positive-noise target-only trajectory with the same label.

\begin{theorem}[Trained flow]\label{an04ctev1:early}
For every closed label interval $I\Subset(0,\pi/2)$ there is $C_I$
independent of the cap level, time, and small $q$ such that
\[
 \sup_{\beta\in I}(|\theta-\widehat\theta|+
 |\psi-\widehat\theta|)\le C_I H^2 E .
\]
For all labels $|\log(m/\widehat m)|\le 4H^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Outer donors in physical coordinates.}
Write $U=\sqrt m\,a$, $W=\sqrt m\,b$ for the two vectors, with
$U'=R^TW$, $W'=RU$, $R=A_\theta-C_f$. Put
$B=\sup_\alpha\max(|U_2|,|W_2|)$. P earlybounds yields
$|U|=|W|\le 2H$ on this cap. For every receiver,
$|(A_\theta)_{12}|\le \mathbb E|X_1X_2|\le Cq$ by the two Gaussian
cluster laws, and $(A_\theta)_{22}\le b_*$. For the full fitted field
$f=\int W(U^TX)_+d\lambda_0$, pointwise
\[
 |f_1|\le 2H(2H|X_1|+B|X_2|),\qquad
 |f_2|\le 2HB|X|.
\]
The gate indicator is bounded by one. Consequently, simultaneously
for all receiver angles,
\begin{equation}\label{an04ctev1:donor}
 |(C_f)_{12}|+|(C_f)_{21}|\le C(HB+qH^2),\qquad
 \|C_f\|\le C H^2 .
\end{equation}
The second-component equations now imply the scalar upper comparison
\[
 D^+B\le(b_*+CH^2)B+CqH(1+H^2).
\]
Equivalently integrate each label's scalar inequalities then take
the supremum. The initial supremum is $\sqrt{S_0}$ and
$\int_0^t H^2\le H(t)^2/(2a_*)$. Gronwall and $a_*-b_*=\omega$
give $B\le C H E$ (the inhomogeneous convolution divided by $H$
is at most $Cq/\omega$). Thus each off-diagonal fitted projection
in \eqref{an04ctev1:donor} is bounded by $C H^2 E$.
This includes left-facing, large scaled angle and endpoint donors.

\emph{Alignment.} Write $d=\psi-\theta$ on the early lifts.
Its target velocity is $-\operatorname{tr}(A_\theta)\sin d$.
The absolute fitted contribution is at most
$C H^2 E+C H^2|d|$: at $b=a$ its antisymmetric contraction
is bounded by the two off-diagonal entries, and changing $b$ costs
$C\|C_f\||d|$. P earlybounds gives $|d|<\pi$, so the target
contributes nonpositively to $D^+|d|$. With $d(0)=0$, integration
gives $|d|\le C H^2 E$ since $2a_*-\omega$ is bounded below.
The radial assertion is P targetcomparison, whose log bound is
$2S_0(2e^{2a_*t}-3e^{a_*t}+1)\le4H^2$ under the same guard.

\emph{Q1 receiver.} For $I$ choose a fixed $\beta_+\in(\sup I,\pi/2)$.
For the target flow $0\le\widehat\theta\le\beta_+$: the zero face
points up by the Gaussian term, and the upper face tends to
$-\omega\sin\beta_+\cos\beta_+<0$ as $q\to0$, uniformly in $\rho$.
For $w=\tan\widehat\theta/q$, CFS's exact equation is
\[
 w'=-\omega w-\tfrac12qwD K(-1/(qD))
          +\tfrac\rho2 D K(-\rho w/D),\qquad D=(1+q^2w^2)^{1/2}.
\]
Here $D\le\sec\beta_+$, hence $w'\le-\omega w+C_I$ and
$|\sin\widehat\theta|\le C_I E$ from time zero.
This proves the required outer-to-inner estimate along a moving label,
without imposing a bounded scaled chart at initialization.

Set $e=\theta-\widehat\theta$ and $v_0(\theta)=a^TA_\theta a^\perp$.
P targetcomparison gives $|v_0'|\le a_*$ globally. In
$\theta'=b^T(A_\theta-C_f)a^\perp$, replacing $b$ by $a$
costs $C|d|$. The remaining fitted term is bounded by
$C H^2(E+|\sin\theta|)$: the diagonal part carries
$\sin\theta\cos\theta$, the off-diagonals use \eqref{an04ctev1:donor}.
Since $|\sin\theta|\le C_I E+|e|$,
\[
 D^+|e|\le(a_*+CH^2)|e|+C_I H^2E .
\]
The homogeneous factor is at most $C e^{a_*(t-s)}$.
Convolving from zero costs a bounded constant since
$2a_*-\omega-a_*=b_*>0$. This proves the estimate for both angles.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Nominal clock and a measured mass level (trained flow).}
\label{an04ctev1:clock}
At $T_A=\log(A/S_0)=\ell/\omega+\log(A/c)$,
$\ell=\log(1/q)$, first fix $A>0$ with $2A<\delta_0$, then send $q$
to zero. $H^2=Ae^{2q^2T_A}$ and $E/q=1+(c/A)^\omega$.
CFS laws gives on $I$, for both trained angles divided by $q$,
\begin{equation}\label{an04ctev1:profile}
 H_b^{-1}((c/A)^\omega\tan\beta)
       +O_I(A[1+(c/A)^\omega])+o_{q;A,I}(1).
\end{equation}
Also $m/(A\cos^2\beta)=1+O(A)+o_{q;A,I}(1)$.
The big-O constants are uniform for the stated $c,\rho$.
The small-noise error is taken at fixed cap.
In particular the angular error divided by $q$ vanishes in the
iterated limit, at order $A^{1-\omega}$ or better.

To specify a measured clock, let $t_\delta$ be the first time the
\emph{fixed right-half ancestry} has radial mass $\delta$.
For all sufficiently small fixed $\delta$, set $A_0=4\delta$.
Then for small $q$ this clock exists and
$t_\delta=T_{A_0}+O(\delta)+o_{q;\delta}(1)$.
Indeed no hit occurs before $T_{\delta/2}$ by the global envelope.
On the subsequent compact range of nominal $A$ from $\delta/2$ to
$5\delta$, the RMS join comparison mass is $A/4+o_{q;\delta}(1)$
uniformly (CFS and RPS sector laws with endpoint strips).
The uniform log-weight bound in Theorem~\ref{an04ctev1:early} gives
relative error at most $C\delta+o_{q;\delta}(1)$ there.
Continuity and bracketing $A_0$ by factors $1\pm C'\delta$
with $C'$ sufficiently large bracket the first hit, including all
earlier times. Applying \eqref{an04ctev1:profile} at the hit
with $A=S_0e^{t_\delta}$ is legitimate by compact uniformity
at fixed $\delta$. One may also use $A_0$ in its place:
the derivative of $H_b^{-1}((c/A)^\omega\tan\beta)$ in $\log A$
is $-[x-\rho K(\rho x)]/2$ at the inverse point $x$.
It is $O_I(1+A^{-\omega})$ by the uniform CFS large-input estimate
$H_b(x)/x\to1$. Thus the same $O_I(\delta^{1-\omega}+\delta)$
angle error and $O(\delta)$ relative weight error hold at this
measured mass with the corresponding fixed-cap vanishing errors.
Current chart membership and right-half ancestry are distinct.

\paragraph{Sharp target and stopping inequality.}\label{an04ctev1:sharp}
The reviewer mass integral is correct for the leading logistic growth
from $M_0$ to $\delta<1/2$:
$\int M\,dt=\log[(1-M_0)/(1-\delta)]$. The source-one correction
is $Mx/2$, hence relative. Even the source-two donor $-\rho S/2$
has no cap-independent $O(M)$ estimate on the proposed profiles.
Take the aligned RMS comparison shape prescribed at mass $M=A/4$.
For $x,\tilde x\ge0$ restriction to the event that the Gaussian is
positive gives $F(\rho x,\rho\tilde x)\ge\rho\tilde x/2$.
The RMS mass from $1\le u\le2$ has fixed positive normalized size
and $H_b^{-1}((c/A)^\omega u)>(c/A)^\omega u$. Thus
$S/M\ge c_1(c/A)^\omega$ even at a fixed nonnegative receiver.
This is an instantaneous counterexample to a uniform per-mass bound
on these prescribed leading data; signed cancellation in the trained
displacement remains possible.

For specificity, the exact scalar input error at $t=t_\delta$ is
\begin{align*}
 e(t)&=\int_0^t e^{\int_s^t h(r)dr}\,
 \{(b-a)^T A_\theta a^\perp-b^TC_f a^\perp\}(s)\,ds,\\
 h&=\int_0^1 v_0'(\widehat\theta+z e)\,dz .
\end{align*}
The stronger $O(\delta)$ scaled discrepancy from target at the
same clock needs
\begin{equation}\label{an04ctev1:missing}
 \limsup_{q\downarrow0}\sup_{\beta\in I}
  \frac{\max(|e(t_\delta)|,|e(t_\delta)+d(t_\delta)|)}q
  \le C_I\delta
\end{equation}
uniformly as $\delta\downarrow0$. For a comparator fixed at $A_0$,
the signed clock displacement must be added before taking the norm.
The proved upper bound costs the factor $1+(c/A)^\omega$;
neither it nor the donor counterexample refutes the sharper trajectory
bound. The weaker rate already removes the original absolute-angle
obstruction for compact Q1 in the stated iterated limit.

\paragraph{Saturation interface.}\label{an04ctev1:sat}
CFS sat evolves a prescribed compact strong measure, $N=0$,
$0<m_0<1/2$, to elapsed time $\log[(1-m_0)/m_0]$ at half mass.
Its bounds depend on support radius and elapsed time.
Our estimate supplies compact Q1 input, output and radial data at
early right-half mass. Three further contracts are distinguishable.
An OWPT-type weighted entry and outer matching estimate must cover Q4,
endpoint strips, Q2/Q3 and their full donor projections, or bound the
error of removing them. A MOMC-type critical mass-clock estimate must
propagate that field error, radial weights and clock to half mass
with constants controlled as the early level tends to zero.
A FWC-type continuation must transport those errors through saturation
on the reached charts and bound the background in the actual
observables. Finite-time clamping at entry pays only the initial
moments. Through the logarithmically increasing saturation delay one
must also pay the integrated propagated error. The critical
$T_A=\ell/\omega+O_A(1)$ is earlier than the deep all-label
$2\ell/\omega+C$ overlap, so ten-power contracts cannot be imported.
These are proposed proof tasks; saturation transfer remains open.
