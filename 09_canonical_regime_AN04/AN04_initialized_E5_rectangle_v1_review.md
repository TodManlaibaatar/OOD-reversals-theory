## Clock, margin and coherent minimum

1. **Same limiting response and phase.** On WIT's coherent leading state the strong axis gives zero at $\rho e_2$, and the weak axis gives $f_2(\rho e_2)=\rho n_\rho$. The normalized weak regression therefore equals $n_\rho$. WIT's coherent scalar is originally defined as radial mass. Its finite-noise compact-state comparison is **inc:AN06:v2:staticbounds**. On the trained flow the observable is instead derived directly as $n_q=\mathbb E_2[D_2f_2]/Q$. Uniform axis separation yields $n_q'=Q n_q(1-n_q)+o(1)$ on every compact centered interval. Centering at its *measured* first half response gives initial value $1/2$ exactly. Thus its limiting response and the WIT half-mass-centered scalar solve the identical IVP $N'=\rho^2 N(1-N)$, $N(0)=1/2$. Both equal $(1+e^{-\rho^2 s})^{-1}$. The relative shift in this limiting comparison is zero. WIT **inc:AN06:v2:orbit, inc:AN06:v2:clockalignment** distinguish this from a finite-noise assertion.
2. **Loss slack.** WIT **inc:AN06:v2:uniformmargins** at the common $s_*\in J$ gives $N_\rho(s_*)\ge1/4+\sigma/2$. Hence $(1-N_\rho(s_*))^2\le(3/4-\sigma/2)^2<9/16=441/784<676/784=169/196$. The two initialized ratios tend uniformly to $0$ and $(1-N_\rho(s_*))^2$. One small-$q$ threshold suffices for both ratios and both response guards.
3. **Two interiority statements.** The fixed observation point $s_*$ lies in the interior of $J=[s_*-r,s_*+r]$ by $r>0$. For each parameter the continuous coherent error attains its minimum $e_*(\rho,\zeta)$ on $J$. Its negative derivative on $J_-$ excludes the left endpoint and its positive derivative on $J_+$ excludes the right endpoint. Thus every coherent minimizer is interior. This gives precisely the comparison value needed at the trained time $T$; off center the minimizing coherent offset may differ from $s_*$. The compact coherent formula bounds $|e_*|$ uniformly.

## Claim checks and the four v1.48 corrections

- **Uniform entrance.** For a varying parameter sequence, take a convergent subsequence and match the incoming field at fixed nominal caps. The early Gaussian comparisons retain $e^{-\omega t}=q(c/A)^\omega$ at the *actual* ratio. The finite-window limit has the correct radial marginal, Q4 bottom path, Q1 incoming coefficient and Q2 seed at the limiting ratio. Then apply the common resident moment bounds. Compactness of the parameter rectangle alone would not justify this step. See **an04e5rv1:matching**.
- **Mass, half response and learning.** The cone inequalities cover both signed horizontal faces, both difference faces, $v=0$ and intersections. The stronger exponent is spent only through the bounded-mass stop; loss controls continuation beyond it. The common determinant gap and charged-energy comparison yield the centered measured weak response. The coherent quarter margin is used only after identifying its phase. See **an04e5rv1:conelemma, an04e5rv1:gap, an04e5rv1:axislemma, an04e5rv1:logistic, an04e5rv1:witness**.
- **Signed prefix.** Both shrinking-band tests use the physical finite-$q$ equations. Their error estimates hold on contrary sequences whose parameters vary. Their final lower overlap has two factors of band width, one from horizontal amplitude and one from label measure. The matched growing and decaying exponentials are combined at the same ratio. See **an04e5rv1:damping, an04e5rv1:bandlemma**.
- **Retained entrance (fix 1) and left weight (fix 4).** The right horizontal entrance converges in $L^2(dl)$ on fixed interiors, exactly what Chebyshev uses. The omitted set is charged by physical weight in the norm of its time supremum. The left entry uses $O(q(1+\ell))=O(q\ell)$; this also suffices when integrating its difference mode. The terminally active left set is traced backwards. See **an04e5rv1:bulklemma, an04e5rv1:left, an04e5rv1:activeleft**.
- **Rank and Gaussian gate (fixes 2–3).** At fixed truncation the minimum compact upper rank $f_\delta$ is positive. At late fixed $b$ it exceeds $F_b$, so the TBW upper input face applies before passing to finite noise. For the incoming deep gate, condition on $|D_2-\rho|\le q$. On this slab the horizontal standard normal is independent of $D_2$, and the uniform lower gate estimate has exponent strictly below $\gamma$. The stopped centered argument charges $T_2-h$ on each of the three physical label sets and excludes a first exit across the enlarged face. See **an04e5rv1:entry, an04e5rv1:gatelemma, an04e5rv1:t2**.
- **Endpoint.** The positive horizontal probe component covers the cosine loss on the deep face. Thus $p_T\ge d_g q k_\alpha\sqrt\ell$ with the same $d_g$. Net favorable signed output divided by $q^2$ dominates the $O(\ell)$ horizontal cost even after charging every adverse label. The exact quadratic term is retained, and $e_*(\rho,\zeta)$ is bounded on the whole rectangle. See **an04e5rv1:criterion, an04e5rv1:endpoint, an04e5rv1:main**.

All premises are analytic. The extension is submitted for independent mathematical review.
