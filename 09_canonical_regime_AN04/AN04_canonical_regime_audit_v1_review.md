## First entry lemmas

**Comparison seed.** On \(\alpha=\pi/2+d_0\), \(0<d_-\le d_0\le d_+<\pi/2\), at \(T=\kappa\ell+O(1)\),
\[
 \widehat\theta-\pi/2=(1+o(1))\tan(d_0)e^{-\lambda T/2},\qquad
 \widehat m=(1+o(1))S_0e^{\lambda T}\sin^2\alpha.
\]
Q2 outer with \(L=\ell\) gives both projected margins greater than \(2qL\) and log-coordinate error \(T[3q^2/4+K(-L)/(2L)]=o(1)\). The negative weak coordinate has size \(q^{-1+\kappa\lambda/2}\) and the radial mass power is \(\kappa(1-\lambda)\). Both boundary labels are excluded.

**Original seed gap.** A sufficient use of Q2 originaltracking requires \(H_Q+\Delta\) below half the comparison displacement along the labels, with \(L_Q(T)=O(1)\) and a matched actual entry time. Its weighted full-output defect is unpaid. The existing absolute early error allowance of order \(\delta\) at fixed cap exceeds the terminal angle scale. ARR's backward terminal-sector reservoir does not identify this bulk cohort at saturation.

**Weak receiver.** With donors and output coordinate fixed,
\[
 2\partial_u u'=-(u+Y)\phi(u)-(1-M)v\phi(u)-\lambda(1-N)
                 -\phi(u)\int(r-u)_+\,d\mu_w(r).
\]
The donor derivative has the favorable sign for positive measures. For \(Y\ge0\) and \((1-M)v\ge0\), the negative-input rate is at least \([\lambda(1-N)-\phi(1)]/2\), and on \([-Y,0]\) at least \(\lambda(1-N)/2\). For \(N\le1/4\) the reduced rate exceeds \(107/3200\). Otherwise its positivity needs checking. For an unknown mass-deficit sign, subtract \(\phi(0)[-(1-M)v]_+/2\) from the lower rate. A time-integrated charge controls distortion. Distinct receiver outputs introduce a separate coupling. The slope alone supplies no inward velocity at zero or at the moving face \(-Y\).
