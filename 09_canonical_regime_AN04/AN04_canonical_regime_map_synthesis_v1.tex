\begin{abstract}
The nominal strong clock distinguishes three questions: which labels have
reached an angular scale in comparison dynamics, which split states evolve
in the leading equations, and which states are reached from training
initialization. At the critical scale, the positive-noise right-half
comparison has a resident atom and a distributed strong component. As
prescribed leading data this law exhibits a gate threshold, finite-time
escape, and survival of an initially inactive mass of order at least
\(1/(1+T)\). Neither this comparison law nor its subsequent weak passage
has been transferred to the trained critical family. We collect the
existing results and their interfaces; the proofs below concern only
their composition.
\end{abstract}

\section{The entry measure and the model change}\label{an04rmsv1:entry}
\begin{lemma}[Join the sectors, then prescribe mass]\label{an04rmsv1:join}
At \(T_A\), the raw positive-noise right-half comparison law has mass
\(A/4\) on the aligned diagonal. As a leading initial shape of mass
\(M_0\) it has test integral \(M_0\langle j\rangle_b\), where
\[
 \langle j\rangle_b=\tfrac12j(a)+\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty
             j(H^{-1}(bu))(1+u^2)^{-2}\dd u .
\]
Its limiting active fraction at offsets in a compact subset of
\((a,\infty)\) is \(\tfrac12+\tfrac12 F_b(H(\zeta))\).
\src{CFS laws, active; RPS q4, fraction; GOE integration}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the zero-noise point-data comparison, a compact Q1 label \(\beta\)
has scaled angle \(b\tan\beta+o(1)\), and weight
\(A\cos^2\beta(1+o(1))\). The Q1 density in its scaled coordinate is
\(g_b(h)=A(1+(h/b)^2)^{-2}/(2\pi b)\) on \(h>0\), and its active
fraction is \(F_b(l)=4\pi^{-1}\int_0^{l_+/b}(1+u^2)^{-2}du\) at \(l=\zeta\).
In the positive-noise comparison the weight is the same but the angle
tends to \(H^{-1}(b\tan\beta)\), with
\[
 B(x)=[x-\rho K(\rho x)]/2,\qquad
 H(x)=x\exp\left[-\omega\int_x^\infty(B(r)^{-1}-(\omega r)^{-1})dr\right]
 \quad(x>a).
\]
CFS proves \(H(x)<x,\ H(x)/x\to1,\ H'>0\), and Q1 density
\(g_b(H(x))H'(x)\). RPS uses the same label law, critical clock,
Gaussian model and radial normalization on Q4. On compact Q4
interiors \(a-\widehat\theta/q\asymp q^{d(1/\omega-2)}\);
Q4 converges to the atom at \(a\), of mass \(A/8\).
Endpoint strips of label length \(\eta\) cost \(O(A\eta)\), and
the shared axis label has zero mass. Adding the Q1 mass \(A/8\),
then dividing by \(A/4\), gives the displayed probability integral
and active fraction. The atom is strictly active and Q1 has no gate
atom. For completeness, in the point-data model the Q4 scaled angle
tends to zero and its mass tends to \(A/8\); hence for the offsets here
the corresponding right-half fraction is \(\tfrac12[1+F_b(\zeta)]\)
by the same weighting \src{CFS profiles}. The Gaussian and point-data
fractions use different Q1 input maps. Q2 and Q3 were never in this denominator. Multiplication by
\(M_0\) prescribes mass without changing the fraction. This last
operation is an assignment of leading data, not a proof of a
trained mass or a trajectory arriving at a prescribed time.
\end{proof}

\section{Resident dynamics and initial gates}\label{an04rmsv1:package}
Write \(F(t,r)=\E[(Z+r)_+1_{Z>-t}],\
 S(x)=\int F(\rho x,\rho\tilde x)d\mu_s,\
 K_s=\int K(\rho x)d\mu_s,\ X_m=\int x\,d\mu_s\).
At \(N=0,\ 0<M\le1\), normalized rank weights are constant.
In one self-consistent leading field both off-diagonal receiver
derivatives are \(\lambda\Phi(\rho x)/2\), so co-order is preserved.
The current active ranks form an initial segment (equality excluded).
For ordered pairs the output gap satisfies
\(\Delta y(t)\ge\sqrt{M_0/M(t)}\Delta y(0)\).
At the exactly saturated coherent resident, centered aligned and
antisymmetric infinitesimal perturbations have rates
\(+\alpha_{\rm sh},-\alpha_{\rm sh}\) while the mean block attracts.
Through half mass from \(0<M_0<1/2\), the isolated source-1 multiplier
is \(\sqrt{M_0/M}\). CFS sat bounds the full-field displacements on
that finite interval for a prescribed compact support. These different
statements neither condense a finite ordered profile nor give it a
universal exponential growth rate. \src{CFS sat, spread; RPS rank}
For large cutoff \(R>\zeta,\ H(R)\ge R/2\) with this bound also
above \(R\), the raw discarded mass and first moment per coordinate
are at most \(Ab^3/[6\pi H(R)^3]\) and \(Ab^3/[2\pi H(R)^2]\).
Clamping onto \(R\) preserves the initial active fraction. These
are instantaneous costs. \src{RPS rank}

With \(U=x'\), the exact moments and exposure \(A_\zeta=\int(\zeta-x)_+d\mu_s\)
obey \src{RPS moments}
\begin{align*}

 \int[\rho\phi(\rho x)-\rho S(x)+\lambda y\Phi(\rho x)]d\mu_s,\\

 A_\zeta'&=(1-M)A_\zeta-\int_{x<\zeta}U\,d\mu_s\quad\text{a.e.}
\end{align*}
At a gate atom the right derivative adds \(\int_{x=\zeta}(-U)_+d\mu_s\).
For the unclamped law the finite-time first-moment domination and
compact-rank limiting construction are supplied by IMP scope and mass;
this does not bound the propagation error of any fixed clamp.

\paragraph{Gate threshold.}
For aligned entry put \(t=\rho\zeta,\ S_b=\langle F(t,\rho x)\rangle_b\).
GOE shows \(S_b\) increases continuously and strictly from \(a/\rho\)
to infinity as \(b\) increases. At mass one, \(b_c\) is the unique
root \(S_b=K(t)\), continuous in \((\rho,\zeta)\) and strictly increasing
in \(\zeta\). Input motion is outward below, stationary at and inward
above the root. For \(0<M_0\le1\) there is an outward range only for
\(M_0>(\zeta-\rho K(t))/(\zeta-a)\); then its upper endpoint solves
\[
 S_b=\frac{\rho K(t)-(1-M_0)\zeta}{\rho M_0}.
\]
At smaller or equal masses motion is inward for each \(b>0\).
Here and below \(S_b\) denotes the unit-mass donor, not \(M_0 S_b\).
\src{RPS face; GOE integration, threshold}

\paragraph{Weak moments at entry.}
For \(M>0\), fixed \(N,V\), let
\(D=\rho K(t)-(1-M)\zeta+\lambda(V-N\zeta)\Phi(t)\).
The full aligned gate has \(2U=D-\rho M S_b\): outward below,
stationary at and inward above the unique root if \(D>Ma\);
inward for all \(b>0\) otherwise. Only the weak increment has sign
\(V-N\zeta\). For a general strong receiver the doubled output velocity
is \(-(1-M)y-Y-K_w+\rho(1-N)K(\rho x)\), with
\(K_w=\int K(u)d\mu_w\). This is instantaneous algebra.
For \(0\le N\le1\) and retained co-order, IMP's forced ceiling for
the positive-part vector uses homogeneous largest rate
\[
 -\frac{1-M}{2}+
 \frac{\lambda}{4}\{\sqrt{(Mf)^2+4(1-N)^2}-Mf\}.
\]
Here \(f\) is normalized upper mass. Its forcing vector is
\(\tfrac12(\rho\phi(0)+\lambda V_+,\,
 \rho(1-N)\phi(0)+(Y+K_w)_-)^T\), integrated with the scalar rate
multiplier on a chart-valid interval. In particular \(2Y'=(1-2M)Y
-MK_w+\rho(1-N)K_s\). A homogeneous ceiling or an initial sign cannot
replace this forcing integral through passage.
\src{IMP weak, matrix, ceiling; GOE threshold}

\paragraph{Finite-time crossings.}
RPS's sufficient compact-entry test is
\(g=\rho K(t)-\max(\rho K_s(0),Y(0))-(1-M_0)(\zeta+1)>0\).
For the normalized unclamped profile the test itself holds precisely
when \(M_0>(\zeta+1-\rho K(t))/(\zeta+1-a)\) and \(b<b_g\),
where \(M_0\langle x\rangle_{b_g}=\rho K(t)-(1-M_0)(\zeta+1)\).
GOE threshold evaluates this test; RPS cone proves its moving-region
statement with compact entry. For an aligned critical clamp \(R>\zeta\)
in the RPS range, let \(C=10e^7(1+\int(|x|+|y|)d\mu_0)\),
\(k=g/[8(\zeta+1)]\),
\(h=\min(1,g/(4C),\log[(\zeta+1)/\zeta]/k)\), and
\(\delta=\min(g/(4\lambda),(\zeta-a)/2,\zeta(1-e^{-kh/2})/2)\).
The band \(\zeta-\delta\le x=y<\zeta\) crosses by \(h/2\) and stays
out through \(h\), with lower envelope \(x(0)e^{kt}\) in elapsed time.
Its normalized mass is \(4A^{-1}\int_{\zeta-\delta}^\zeta
g_b(H(u))H'(u)\,du\). This distinguishes newly inactive labels
from those inactive from entry. \src{RPS cone, moved; GOE threshold}

\begin{lemma}[Ceiling, escape and survival use different quantifiers]
\label{an04rmsv1:horizons}
For the unit-mass unclamped law at \(N=0\), both the GOE escape
obstruction and the IMP survival estimate apply. More explicitly,
for a fixed rank of upper mass \(f>0\),
\[
 \max(x_+,y_+)\le C_fe^{r_+(f)t},\qquad
 r_+(f)=\frac{\lambda}{4}(\sqrt{4+f^2}-f)<\frac\lambda2.
\]
No fixed positive upper segment can maintain \(x\ge c_0e^{rt}\),
\(c_0,r>0\), on intervals of unbounded length starting at a fixed time.
Nevertheless for each finite \(T\),
\[
 \mu_0\{x(t)\ge\zeta\ \text{for all }0\le t\le T\}
 \ge C_{b,\rho,\zeta}/(1+T).
\]
\src{GOE ceiling, escape, gap, failure; IMP budget, mass}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{an04rmsv1:join} at \(M_0=1\) supplies the normalized
entry with positive \(Y(0)\), upper-tail density asymptotic to
\(2b^3/(\pi x^4)\) and strictly ordered continuous upper ranks.
Thus the first two absolute moments are finite. With \(N=0\)
the mean equation preserves \(Y\ge0\), as required by GOE.
The GOE obstruction uses two different interior ranks; it does not
apply to a passive test label or the last atom of a clamp.
For comparison, at fixed horizon \(h\), \(D_0=1+\int(|x|+|y|)d\mu_0\),
and \(0<f<1/2\) with entry quantile
\(\lambda\xi_f>2D_0 e^{7h}\), GOE escape guarantees both coordinates
at least \(\xi_f e^{r_- t}\) through \(h\), with
\(r_-=(\lambda-2D_0e^{7h}/\xi_f)/2>0\).

IMP integrates the same ceiling against this actual tail before
constructing its lower barrier. Its protected labels start above
\(R_T=\zeta+1+3B[(1+T)^{1/3}-1]\) for a fixed donor-budget constant
\(B\); their mass is bounded below by order \((1+T)^{-1}\).
The labels stay fixed on the given interval but the chosen set
varies with \(T\). This matches both source hypotheses and proves
compatibility without taking a positive limit of the lower bound.
At \(T=2\ell/\lambda\) this is an inverse-logarithmic guarantee
in the zero-weak-mass evolution. The logistic switch time with
that coefficient would require the candidate weak seed.
\end{proof}

For a finite fixed rank a first hit of \(R_q\) takes at least
\((\log R_q-O_f(1))/r_+(f)\), if it occurs. At
\(\log R_q=\ell+o(\ell)\) this is later in leading coefficient than
the candidate \(2\ell/\lambda\). GOE's isolated coherent-field
passive example attains coefficient \(2/\lambda\). These clocks
are neither a uniform approximation on growing charts nor a prediction
of arrival of positive mass in the weak passage. \src{GOE large, clock}

\paragraph{Return and lower ranks.}
Initially \(b>b_c\) gives short-time return of some initially
inactive labels, and \(b<b_c\) gives new outward crossings. These are
regular aligned gates with positive Q1 density. The survivor inequality
holds on both sides. For the bottom Q4 atom the exact equations are

\]
Both derivatives are negative initially for all \(b>0\), since
\(m=\langle x\rangle_b>a\). For \(C=100e^7,\ h=1/(4C)\) and
\(m>\max(1,4a,8a/(\lambda h))\), the atom crosses \(x=0\) before
\(h\) and stays negative through \(h\). GOE gives \(m/b\to1/\pi\);
this is a sufficient large-scale range, with no complementary
capture claim. \src{GOE integration, threshold; IMP bottomfield, mass}
