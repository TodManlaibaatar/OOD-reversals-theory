# E5 Step 6 · signed probe motion

**Decision.** Partial for the actual initialized critical family. There is a favorable longitudinal input-rotation term through the first weak half-response hit. The transverse equation contains a cross-output regression multiplied by a positive gate weight. Its accumulated positive part cannot be treated as a bounded scaled error. The net learned-depth comparison remains open.

**What is new for the trained family.**

1. From a fixed COD witness, at each subsequent fixed resident delay the longitudinal motion has lower limit at least $k e^{Ls}/(1+s)-C\sqrt{1+s}$, and the absolute transverse motion has upper limit at most $C\sqrt{1+s}$. The bounds are uniform over extracted laws, taking small noise at fixed time. See `an04spmv1:early`.
2. At every actual time, write the full weak-cluster residual as its vector regression against the signed data coordinate in the probe direction plus the orthogonal remainder. In the witness basis the parallel coefficient is $\alpha=1-\mathsf m_2+O(q)$ and the perpendicular coefficient is $\delta=\mathbb E_{P_2}[D_2 f_1]/(\rho^2+q^2)+O(q)$. The formulas use the exact coefficients rather than integrating these approximations. Before half response, $\alpha\ge1/3$ for small noise. The direct longitudinal input-rotation term $Q\alpha j_{uu}/2$ is nonnegative. The transverse input terms are $Q(\alpha j_{vu}+\delta j_{vv})/2$. The overlap matrix is positive semidefinite, with no fixed sign for its mixed entry. Strong regression and both centered residuals are included in `an04spmv1:sources`, `an04spmv1:centered`, and `an04spmv1:one`.
3. For every sequence of reached half-response hits or learned times as noise vanishes, both $\int\delta_+j_{vv}/q$ and $\int \delta^2 j_{vv}/q^2$ diverge from the fixed witness. The first is the positive variation of a particular transverse source, up to the constant $Q/2$. The second is the penalty that appears on absorbing a mixed longitudinal term by a squared bound. See `an04spmv1:divergence`. This result makes no assumption about the sign of either *net* motion.

**Why the divergent budget is an initialized result.** The physical cross regression on the weak cluster, divided by $q$, tends to $K_s/\rho$ on each reached resident interval. The protected upper donors give $K_s/\rho\ge c_3 e^{Ls}/(1+s)$. The Q4 core remains probe-active and still overlaps the weak Gaussian gate. Retaining the polynomial factor in the bottom-input equation and applying the Gaussian upper tail on the lower face gives $x_a\ge-\sqrt{s-2\log(1+s)/(3L)+d}$ at late resident times. The entry into the single lower face is strict, and its inward velocity is checked in the proof. The lower Gaussian tail then gives $j_{vv}\gtrsim e^{-Ls}(1+s)^{1/6}$ in the fixed-time lower limit. The product for the positive transverse term is at least a constant times $(1+s)^{-5/6}$ and is not integrable. The proof uses finite horizons first, Fatou's lemma second, and only then arbitrarily long horizons. No approximation is evaluated at a diverging weak clock.

**Independent check using the measured response.** Regression on the exact parameter gradient of a weak response in probe coordinates gives an interval-wide signed center for each motion and a joint Cauchy--Schwarz bound on the errors. One term in the longitudinal center is a nonnegative gate square. A weak-cluster output-weight term costs at most $Cq^2$ per unit mass per time before scaling. On a fixed pre-weak unit interval the initialized strong response grows by a fixed positive amount; a definite part of the weak-response gradient sits on an inactive Q1 band while the probe gradients see an active Q4 band. Thus both projections retain uncertainty radii at least $d/q$ in the norm estimate, even after subtracting the measured response and its dissipation cost. These are radii of an upper-bound method, not actual errors. See `an04spmv1:measured`, `an04spmv1:split`, `an04spmv1:ellipse`, and `an04spmv1:width`.

**Exact remaining sign.** With the definitions in the note, the unpaid interval for the signed longitudinal remainder is `an04spmv1:missing`. Its width depends on the actual transverse sum, including the mixed gate overlap and the transverse projection of `an04spmv1:residual`. That residual contains the entire strong-cluster update and the part of the weak update not accounted for by the measured response mode. A proof must pay their signed combination against the positive longitudinal gain. A large positive transverse source could be canceled by another term or outweighed by longitudinal gain. The learned window itself has not been shown to be reached at critical initialization.

**Scope and verification.** The fixed $c,\rho,\zeta$ and the fixed COD witness precede the small-noise limit. The signed field bounds are exact or Gaussian inequalities along the trained trajectory; the large-budget conclusions are conditional only on the indicated hits being reached. No substitute initial state, simulated trajectory, or deep-family weak-passage estimate is used.
