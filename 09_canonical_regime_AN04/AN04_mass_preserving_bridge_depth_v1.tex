\paragraph{Leading system and depth convention.}
Fix $(\rho,\zeta)$ in WIT's learned rectangle. Use its selected
coherent orbit $z_0(s)=(X,Y,U,V)(s)$, strong mass one, and weak
mass $n=(1+e^{-\lambda s})^{-1}$, $\lambda=\rho^2$.
Depth here refers to the minimum of the leading scaled probe error on
the fixed learned window $J$. WIT uniformtheorem gives $X<\zeta$
there with a strict margin and strictly negative and positive rates
on the left and right endpoint subwindows. A minimizer $s_m$ on $J$
therefore exists in its interior. Fix any such minimizer. Write
\[
 A=\zeta-X_m,\quad Z=X_m-Y_m,\quad
 P=\Phi(\rho X_m),\quad Q=\Phi(U_m),
 \qquad z_m=z_0(s_m).
\]
Here $A>0$, and AN01 post-half-mass barrier, label
\texttt{an01pbv1:sign}, gives $Z>0$ on this branch.
No uniqueness or nondegeneracy of the minimum is used.

\begin{theorem}[Prescribed split, full leading dynamics]\label{an04mpbv1:depth}
At $s_0=s_m-h\in J$ replace strong mass $\varepsilon$ of the coherent
atom by a bridge. Its normalized entry law is the image of a probability
$\nu$ on $(r_x,r_y)$ under $(r_x,r_y)\mapsto(r_x/h,r_y/h)$.
Fix $0<a\le K_*<\infty$, and assume support in
$[a,K_*]\times[-K_*,K_*]$. Set
$\kappa_x=\int r_x\dd\nu$, $\kappa_y=\int r_y\dd\nu$.
Leave the weak state unchanged at injection and evolve every label
in the common self-consistent field thereafter. Put
\[
 D=\tfrac12\{\lambda P Z\kappa_x+A\kappa_y\}-AZ .
\]
There exist $h_0>0$, $0<\varepsilon_0<1$ and $C<\infty$ such that,
for $0<h<h_0$, $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0$ and every such law,
\begin{equation}\label{an04mpbv1:estimate}
 \big|\EE_{\rm split}(s_m)-\EE_{\rm coh}(s_m)+\varepsilon D\big|
 \le C\varepsilon(h+\varepsilon).
\end{equation}
The constants may depend on the fixed coherent orbit, window, and
support bounds. For $D>0$, choose $h,\varepsilon$ sufficiently small
that $C(h+\varepsilon)<D/2$. Then
\[
 \min_{s_0\le s\le\sup J}\EE_{\rm split}(s)
 \le\EE_{\rm split}(s_m)
 <\min_{s\in J}\EE_{\rm coh}(s)-\varepsilon D/2 .
\]
For $D<0$ the difference at $s_m$ has the opposite strict sign
for sufficiently small $h,\varepsilon$. This last statement does not
order the optimized minima.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use $K(t)=\phi(t)+t\Phi(t)$, $F(t,r)=\phi(\min(t,r))+r\Phi(\min(t,r))$.
WIT popsystem gives $M'=M(1-M)$ and $n'=\lambda n(1-n)$. Thus
$M=1$, the strong label weights are constant, and $n(s)$ and the
weak half-mass clock are identical in both trajectories. The weak atom's
two angular coordinates remain dynamical. Write $z=(x,y,u,v)$ for
the actual core and weak coordinates, $\beta=(b_x,b_y)$ for a moving
bridge label, and an overbar for averaging those labels under $\nu$.
The coherent angular field $G$ at the same time and state is
\[
 2G(s,z)=\begin{pmatrix}
 \lambda\Phi(\rho x)[nv-x+(1-n)y]\\
 -y-nK(u)+\rho(1-n)K(\rho x)\\
 \phi(u)-nK(u)-y\Phi(u)-\lambda(1-n)u\\
 \rho K(\rho x)-\lambda v
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Replacing the donors, while retaining the actual receivers, gives
the following exact equations whenever $b_x\ge x$ throughout the bridge:
\begin{equation}\label{an04mpbv1:field}
 z'=G(s,z)+\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}B(s),\qquad
 B=\begin{pmatrix}
 -\lambda\Phi(\rho x)(\overline{b_x}-x)\\
 -(\overline{b_y}-y)\\
 -\Phi(u)(\overline{b_y}-y)\\
 \rho(\overline{K(\rho b_x)}-K(\rho x))
 \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Indeed $F(\rho x,\rho b_x)-K(\rho x)=\rho(b_x-x)\Phi(\rho x)$
under this order. The output donor is $(1-\varepsilon)y+
\varepsilon\overline{b_y}$ in both the strong and weak receiver
equations. The bridge itself sees the complete strong overlap
$(1-\varepsilon)F(\rho b_x,\rho x)+
\varepsilon\int F(\rho b_x,\rho\widetilde b_x)\dd\nu$,
and the same output and weak donors. This includes its internal motion.

\emph{Uniform displacement and gate checks.}
Let $R(s)$ be the supremum of the absolute values of all coordinates.
From the unsimplified WIT field, $0\le F(t,r)\le K(r)\le\phi(0)+r_+$,
bounded logistic masses and $|\Phi|\le1$ give
$|\text{each velocity}|\le C(1+R)$ for all states. The constant
is independent of $\nu,h,\varepsilon$. The chart equations are locally
Lipschitz on bounded sets: the kernel has continuous partials
$F_t=(r-t)_+\phi(t)$ and $F_r=\Phi(\min(t,r))$. For a compactly
supported continuum these same local bounds apply in the supremum
norm on continuous label maps on the initial support. Integration
and Gronwall give continuation on every finite interval.
Initially $R\le C(1+K_*/h)$. On $I_h=[s_0,s_m]$ this yields
$R\le C'(1+K_*/h)$ and displacement of every coordinate at most
$Ch(1+K_*/h)\le C''$ for $h\le1$. In particular
\[
 b_x=r_x/h+O(1),\qquad b_y=r_y/h+O(1),
\]
uniformly, and $z$ is in a fixed compact box since its initial
value is coherent. For sufficiently small $h$, $b_x\ge a/h-C''$
exceeds both $\zeta$ and the upper edge of the core box. This
establishes the overlap ordering and the inactive bridge gate directly.

On the reached compact core and weak box $G$ has a bounded Lipschitz
constant. Equation~\eqref{an04mpbv1:field} has $|B|\le C/h$, and
$z(s_0)=z_0(s_0)$. Gronwall gives
$\sup_{I_h}|z-z_0|\le C\varepsilon$.
WIT's gate margin now keeps $x<\zeta$ uniformly for small
$\varepsilon$. Both gate checks were obtained from the full equations.

\emph{Integrated response of both populations.}
On $I_h$, $z_0(s)=z_m+O(h)$, so
$\Phi(\rho x)=P+O(h+\varepsilon)$ and
$\Phi(u)=Q+O(h+\varepsilon)$. The positive lower bound for $b_x$
and $0<K(t)-t\le\phi(0)$ for $t\ge0$ yield
$\overline{K(\rho b_x)}=\rho\kappa_x/h+O(1)$.
Also $\overline{b_i}=\kappa_i/h+O(1)$ for each coordinate.
In the integral of \eqref{an04mpbv1:field} subtract the coherent
equation. The $G$ difference integrates to $O(h\varepsilon)$, including
the feedback of both evolving weak coordinates. The $B$ integral
over length $h$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{an04mpbv1:response}
 z(s_m)-z_m=\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}
 (-\lambda P\kappa_x,-\kappa_y,-Q\kappa_y,\lambda\kappa_x)
 +O\big(\varepsilon(h+\varepsilon)\big).
\end{equation}
In particular the weak coordinate changes themselves can be of order
$\varepsilon$. The proof only charges their subsequent action on the
core over the short interval.

\emph{Mass removal and the minimum.}
P probe-pop and WIT generalrates use
$\EE=A_\zeta^2/2-\zeta A_\zeta+\int y(\zeta-x)_+\dd\mu_s$.
The weak probe is inactive in this leading observable.
With the two strict strong gates just proved the exact energy is
\[
 H(\varepsilon,x,y)=\tfrac12(1-\varepsilon)^2(\zeta-x)^2
       +(1-\varepsilon)(y-\zeta)(\zeta-x).
\]
At any active coherent state the instantaneous price of removal is
$\varepsilon(\zeta-X)(X-Y)+\varepsilon^2(\zeta-X)^2/2$.
At $(0,X_m,Y_m)$ the derivatives $(H_\varepsilon,H_x,H_y)$
are $(AZ,Z,A)$. The Hessian is bounded on the reached compact set.
Taylor expansion with \eqref{an04mpbv1:response} gives
\eqref{an04mpbv1:estimate}, including the asserted error uniformity.
Evaluating at the original minimizing time suffices for the strict
depth comparison.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Aligned atom and boundary (leading system).}
\label{an04mpbv1:threshold}
For a single atom at $(\kappa/h,\kappa/h)$ the gain coefficient $D$ is positive
precisely when
\[
 \kappa>\kappa_*=\frac{2AZ}{A+\lambda P Z}.
\]
For instance $\kappa=2Z$ gives $D=\lambda P Z^2>0$.
A compact distribution of diagonal marks with the same mean has
the same leading coefficient. Equality cancels the leading
coefficient at $s_m$. The estimate does not decide equality.

\paragraph{Exact first-variation test at a fixed injection time.}
\label{an04mpbv1:fixed}
For fixed $h$ in the separated range, let $B_0(s)$ denote $B$ in
\eqref{an04mpbv1:field} along the passive bridge trajectories and
coherent core and weak state at $\varepsilon=0$. Put
$c_m=(Z,A,0,0)^T$ and solve the backward linear equation
\[
 -p'=(D_zG(s,z_0(s)))^T p,\qquad p(s_m)=c_m .
\]
Differentiation at fixed $h$ is justified by the continuously
differentiable kernel on the reached bounded set, or directly by
difference quotients, continuous dependence and Gronwall.
The state variation satisfies $w'=D_zG\,w+B_0/2$, $w(s_0)=0$.
No derivative of the passive labels enters the direct forcing since
the moving-donor difference is multiplied by $\varepsilon$.
Integrating $(p^Tw)'$ gives the exact signed test
\[
 \left.\partial_{\varepsilon+}\EE_{\rm split}(s_m)\right|_0
     =AZ+\tfrac12\int_{s_0}^{s_m}p(s)^T B_0(s)\dd s .
\]
Strict first-order improvement at this time holds precisely when
the integral is less than $-2AZ$. This also applies on a longer
fixed interval when the relevant strict gates and overlap order hold.
It retains weak feedback at all times. It does not assert a sign on
long intervals or when the leading short-time coefficient vanishes.

\paragraph{Scope.} The construction prescribes a split at a chosen
time within the learned window and fixes $R=\kappa/h$ before any
finite-noise interpretation. It proves existence of a deeper leading
minimum relative to the coherent learned-window minimum. Deriving
this much donor forcing from an initialized critical profile, transport
through an extended weak passage, and transfer to the finite-noise
probe require separate estimates.
