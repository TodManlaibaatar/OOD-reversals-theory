## Claim-by-claim checks

**1. Charged energy.** Sections 2–3 supply bounded mass, bounded integrated $|1-m|+|n|$, and the sharp clock on every fixed half-centered continuation. They precede the energy argument. For $g_{ij}=\nabla F_{ij}$ the two blocks are $(W_i v_j,e_i b_j)$. Euler's identity $U\cdot v_j=b_j$ gives the diagonal pairing on the two complementary charge sets, including its off-coordinate gate derivative. The leading absolute value is at most $2E_J$ and the remainder costs $Cq\sqrt{E_J}$. In a cross pairing, subtracting $F_{ij}$ leaves an explicit difference of two off-coordinate integrals, bounded by $CqS$. Multiplication by the signed regression produces its negative square plus $O(q\sqrt{E_J}+q^2)$. REC bounds the remaining shape pairing by $Cq\sqrt{E_J}$. This is how the coefficient $Q$ survives division by $2\sqrt{E_J+q^2}$.

For each **fixed** delay $b$ and fixed charged arc, BRW transfers the right label-path law with radial weight $4M(b)\cos^2\alpha$. Fixed-$b$ vertical envelopes pay for auxiliary endpoint strips before taking large $b$. COD's positive-rank majorant is square-integrable against the physical rank measure. Its bottom input bound and the BRW mean budget control both negative parts. TBW's compact Q2 seed leaves precisely a discounted cost proportional to the integral of $\sin^2\alpha$ on the unretained Q2 labels. The order is $q\downarrow0$, then $b\to\infty$, then Q2 exhaustion. Joining the late estimate to $[t_0,t_h+b]$, where all vertical components are $O_b(q)$, gives the asserted supremum over the full interval. Section 4 also checks the positive vertical sum and small difference from the cone through the bounded post-hit continuation before using the weak logistic equation.

**2. Both shrinking bands.** On the contrary prefix set $P_t=\int_{t_0}^t\sup_\alpha(|\mathsf R_{12}|+|\mathsf R_{21}|)$. Equations **prefix** and **earlyh** give $P_t=O(q(1+s))$ or $O(q^{3/4})$, respectively. For either choice, $P_t/\epsilon=o(1)$ and $\epsilon P_t/q=o(1)$. At the literal initialization both components in each layer agree and are positive. Before $t_0$ the horizontal perturbation costs $O(q)$ in $H_0$ units and the vertical costs $O(\epsilon)$ in $V_0$ units. Integrating the weak wrong-gate tail uses $\int_0^\infty\exp[-c e^{2\omega u}/\epsilon^2]du$ and a separate $O(\ell e^{-c/q^2})$ term. This handles arbitrarily slowly increasing delays. The lower constants are chosen before the tail thresholds for $q$.

After $t_0$ the coupled upper bounds are absorbed before testing any lower face. Balance gives horizontal entrance imbalance $O(q^2/\epsilon)$. The vertical pair can have unequal entrance components; the positivity estimate uses $p_0-|d_0|\ge c q$ for its sum and difference modes, together with $\int\mathsf R_{22}\ge-Cqs$. Variation of constants strictly improves the lower faces on every stopped prefix. The final unit contributes at least $c\epsilon^2 q e^{Qs/2}$, including one factor of $\epsilon$ from label measure. With $\epsilon=(1+s)^{-4}$ this contradicts $I=O(q(1+s))$. With $\epsilon=q^{1/3}$ at $t_R-1$ it contradicts $I=O(q^{3/4})$ since $q^{2/3}/q^{3/4}=q^{-1/12}$. The second contradiction is at each fixed sufficiently large $R$. Both calculations use the finite-$q$ characteristic equations throughout.

**3. Retained physical weight.** A disposable cutoff $R'$ gives $\int g=o(1)$ and $\int\|\mathbf1_{A_R}(U_2,W_2)\|\,dt=o(1)$ by first taking $q\to0$ and then $R'\to\infty$. Integrating the vertical equation label by label *before* taking the supremum and label norm proves the stronger time-supremum estimate. On fixed right interiors the horizontal entry is $a k_\alpha$ with $a=2\sqrt{M(b_0)}$. The scalar-diagonal sum mode has a multiplier between $e^{-M}$ and $e^M$, with global $M$. Chebyshev removes errors exceeding a fixed fraction of $k_\alpha$. The compact minimum of $k_\alpha$ enters only the measure threshold for the discarded set. The comparison constant $D$ is independent of $\delta,R,b$. On any right exceptional set the global bounded horizontal entry and the vanishing norm errors bound its physical weight by $C\sqrt{|E|}+o(1)$.

On the left, **left** bounds the time-supremum horizontal norm on each prefix. The vertical difference has a bounded forward propagator because $\mathsf R_{22}\ge-Cq$. On the terminally active subset $p_T>0$ controls the positive part of $U_2(T)$, and the excursion estimate controls the negative part. The vertical sum has a bounded **backward** propagator for the same reason. Integrating it from $T$ on precisely that subset gives **activeleft**. This pays for weak labels that return to the probe.

**4. Centered gate and signed regression.** At fixed truncation and then late fixed $b$, the tagged TBW negative face gives strict entry; the COD rank ceiling and BRW donor moment pay for the compact's earlier weak excitation. The uniform prefix bound pays the later $O(qk_\alpha)$ cost while $U_2\le0$. In particular $W_2\le-c_2 k_\alpha I$. At zero the weighted weak gate is $1/2$. On the final incoming unit its lower tail is at least $c q^\gamma$, $\gamma<1/4$, throughout the strip of normalized width $d_gq\sqrt\ell$. The resulting downward speed crosses the strip in time less than one, uniformly on the retained labels.

On the centered stopped interval, the decomposition of $T_2-h$ charges the retained right, discarded right, and left separately. This gives $(C\sqrt\delta+o(1))I$, including the left $o(1)(q\ell+I)$ term. The propagation of the incoming $h_-$ costs $C e^{-LR}I$ and the new exceptional forcing costs $C_R\sqrt\delta\,I$. With the choices below, $h_-<\kappa I$. The stop uses the *outer* face $-3d_gq\sqrt\ell/4$ in units of $k_\alpha$. Throughout the intervening strip the drift is strictly downward, so the positive part above the inner face stays zero for each retained label. The supremum is Lipschitz and remains separated from the stopping value even when no label realizes it.

**5. Cone and sharp clock.** At $t_0$ the compact Q2 seed and balance place all six signed faces strictly inside. The pre-hit loss budget controls $\|r\|_{P_1}$ and $\|r_1\|_{P_2}$; the full loss bounds $\|r_2\|_{P_2}$. The proof writes the three all-gate entry estimates separately. On the cone the missing weak-cluster term in $\mathsf R_{22}$ costs an exponential Gaussian tail and its strong-cluster term costs $O(q)$. Thus $v'\ge\lambda v/4$; each of $\pm U_1$, $\pm W_1$, $\pm(U_2-W_2)/2$ has outward derivative strictly below $v'/2$ at its face. The $v=0$ face is excluded by positive growing entry. These inequalities also apply at intersections. The bounded-mass estimate up to $\min(t_d,t_\eta)$ improves the exponent to $Q/2-C(E+\sqrt E+q)$. If the hit is later, $v(t_d)\ge k>0$ on a fixed positive-measure arc, and the loss-budget cone continues from there against linear mass growth. If it is earlier, $\eta\le E+Cq\sqrt E$ forces the matching clock lower bound. Equality at the cutoff is covered by the earlier-hit case.
